Our Story

More than a T-shirt.

TOTEM THREADS was created around a simple idea: your heritage deserves to be seen.

We create fashion inspired by African totems, family names, animals and cultural identity. Each design is made to help people wear their story with pride.

From a traditional family totem to a completely personal design, we bring identity and fashion together.
